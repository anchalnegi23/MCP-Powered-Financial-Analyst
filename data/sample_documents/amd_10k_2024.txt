ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES, INC. — FORM 10-K, FISCAL YEAR 2024 (EXTRACT)

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

Reliance on third-party foundries.
We rely on third-party foundry partners to manufacture our products and do not operate
our own fabrication facilities. Capacity constraints, yield problems or price increases
at these partners would impair our ability to supply products and would compress margins.

Intense competition in a consolidating market.
We compete against companies with substantially greater financial resources, larger
research and development budgets and established customer relationships in both the
client computing and data centre markets. Aggressive pricing by competitors has in the
past reduced our margins and could do so again.

Dependence on the personal computer market.
A meaningful portion of our revenue derives from the personal computer market, which has
experienced cyclical and structural decline. Weakness in consumer and commercial PC
demand reduces shipments of our client processors and graphics products.

Execution risk in data centre and AI accelerators.
Our data centre growth depends on timely delivery of competitive accelerator products and
on the maturity of our software ecosystem. Delays in product qualification, or customer
preference for a more established software stack, would slow adoption.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $25,785 million, an increase of 14% from $22,680 million
in fiscal year 2023. Data Center segment revenue grew substantially, offset by a decline
in the Gaming segment. Gross margin was 49%, and operating expenses rose as we increased
investment in artificial intelligence software and hardware engineering.
